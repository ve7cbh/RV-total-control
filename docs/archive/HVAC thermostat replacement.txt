Notes

* Pin 5 (Grn/Yel) and Pin 6 (White) — measure with meter before connecting. 
  May be zone 2 or heat strip — not used on single-zone cooling-only install.

* Yellow (Pin 4) load shed is an INPUT to the A/C unit, not a thermostat output. 
  The A/C shuts itself down when this line is pulled low by shore-power management.
  RVTC already handles load shed via KWS-303L + relay board — wire this pin to GND or leave open (confirm behaviour first).

* Furnace call (Pin 2, Blue) — relay coil 3 switches 12V onto this wire to call for heat. 
  All gas valve and ignition sequencing is internal to the furnace.

* Compressor short-cycle delay — handled by control board K1 inside the A/C unit. 
  No ESPHome interlock required, but a 3-min software guard is good practice.

* Furnace and A/C demand relays must be mutually exclusive in ESPHome — never energise both coils simultaneously.

  There's the proposed wiring. A few things to verify with your meter before connecting anything:
Measure first:

  Pin 1 to Pin 3 → expect ~12VDC (confirms Red = 12V+, Black = GND)
  Pin 2 (Blue) to Pin 3 at rest → should be 0V; call for heat at the old thermostat (if still connected) → should go to 12V
  Pin 4 (Yellow) to Pin 3 → note voltage at rest and when A/C is running 
    — this tells you whether load shed is active-high or active-low
  Pin 5 (Green/Yellow) and Pin 6 (White) → measure to GND in both states 
    — if they stay at 0V in all modes they're probably not connected internally on your unit
